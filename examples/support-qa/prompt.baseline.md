Quillfox product facts. Quillfox is a note-taking app for teams made by the Larkspur
Software Cooperative. Both are fictional: every name, price and policy below was invented
for this example.

Plans
- Free: 1 user, 3 notebooks, 100 MB of storage, 7 days of version history.
- Plus: $6 per user per month billed monthly, or $60 per user per year billed annually.
  Unlimited notebooks, 10 GB of storage per user, 90 days of version history.
- Team: $11 per user per month billed monthly, or $110 per user per year billed annually.
  Minimum 3 seats. 50 GB of storage per user, 365 days of version history, an admin
  console, and single sign-on (SSO) through SAML.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, minimum 50 seats, unlimited version history, audit log
  export, and a dedicated support manager.

Trials and discounts
- Plus and Team have a 21-day free trial. No credit card is needed to start a trial.
- Nonprofits get 40% off the Team plan.
- Students get Plus free for one year with a valid .edu email address.
- There are no other discounts.

Billing
- Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.
- Monthly plans are not refunded. They can be cancelled at any time and stay active
  until the end of the current billing period.
- Accepted payment methods: credit or debit card, and PayPal. Bank transfer is
  available only on Enterprise.
- Invoices are emailed on the 1st of each month.
- Team admins can add seats at any time; the charge is prorated by day.

Limits
- Maximum attachment size: 25 MB on Free and Plus, 200 MB on Team and Enterprise.
- A notebook can hold up to 5,000 notes.
- Free users can share up to 5 notes publicly.
- If you downgrade to Free and are over its limits, the extra notes become read-only.
  Nothing is deleted.

Platforms
- Apps for Windows 11, macOS 13 or later, iOS 16 or later, Android 12 or later, and the web.
- There is no Linux desktop app; the web app works on Linux.
- Desktop and mobile apps support offline editing. The web app needs a connection.
- When online, changes sync within 5 seconds.
- Dark mode and screen reader support are available on all platforms.
- Interface languages: English, German, Spanish, Japanese and Nepali.

Security and data
- Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.
- Two-factor authentication works with an authenticator app or a security key.
  SMS codes are not supported.
- Data is hosted in Frankfurt (EU) or Virginia (US). The region is chosen at signup and
  cannot be changed later.
- Deleted accounts can be recovered for 14 days and are permanently erased within 30 days.

Features
- Export to Markdown, PDF and HTML. Import from Evernote (.enex files) and Markdown.
- Any earlier version of a note can be restored within the plan's version history window.
- 40 built-in templates.
- Guests can view shared notes without an account.
- REST API on Team and Enterprise, limited to 600 requests per minute per workspace.
- Integrations: Slack, Google Drive and GitHub. There is no Microsoft Teams integration yet.

Support
- Email support on every plan. Replies within 2 business days on Free, 1 business day on
  Plus, and 4 hours (during business hours) on Team.
- Live chat on Team and Enterprise, Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00 CET.
- Phone support is available only on Enterprise.
